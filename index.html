About SATA Apparel

Apparel Sourcing Solutions Built Around Your Business.

SATA Apparel is a Bangladesh-based garment sourcing initiative dedicated to connecting international buyers with apparel sourcing and manufacturing solutions.

We aim to support clothing brands, retailers, wholesalers and businesses with a wide range of apparel products, customized requirements and sourcing assistance.

From everyday essentials to specialized clothing, our goal is to make apparel sourcing more accessible, efficient and convenient for buyers around the world.
